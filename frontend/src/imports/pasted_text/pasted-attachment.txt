IMPORTANT INTERACTIONS:

- Clicking "Risk Map" opens the full Risk Map screen.
- Clicking a map location opens the Location Risk Details side panel.
- "Why this Risk?" opens the AI explanation section.
- "View Historical Landslides" shows historical landslide locations near the selected point.
- "Subscribe to Alerts" changes to "Subscribed ✓".
- "Refresh Risk Data" shows a loading state and then updated data.
- Risk filters dynamically change visible markers/heatmap.
- Search Location shows the selected location and prediction.
- "View Details" on alerts opens the location risk panel.
- "Acknowledge" changes alert status to "Acknowledged".
- Field Report submission shows a success confirmation.
